Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

One Thing is a shared to-do app for two people. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to delete it. Short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads or trackers.

What we store

  • What you put in the app: your household's two first names, tasks, notes, events, shopping lists, "Someday, Together" ideas, Stars and the items in your room.
  • A sign-in ID for each phone. There's no email or password: each phone gets a random ID when you start or join a household.
  • A notification token for each phone that turns on notifications, so we can send your reminders.
  • Your time zone, so the day resets at 4 a.m. where you live and reminders arrive on time.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or anything about other apps.

Who can see it

Only the phones that joined your household with its code. Your household code works like a key, so share it only with your partner. If you turn on the calendar link, anyone who has that link can see your events; you can reset it any time in Settings.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States. Notifications go through Apple's push service (on iPhone) or your browser's push service (on the web). Both only see the notification text.

What we don't do

  • No selling or sharing your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No ads, analytics trackers, or tracking across other apps and websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. That deletes your phone's sign-in and notification token right away. If your partner is still in the household, they keep your shared tasks, events and home. If you're the last one, the whole household and everything in it is deleted for good.

Children

One Thing is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.
