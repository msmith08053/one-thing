One Thing support

One task each, every day: a shared to-do app for couples, with events, shared lists and a cozy home you build together.

Common questions

How does my partner join? Open Settings, copy your household code, and send it to them. On their phone: open One Thing → I have a code.

New phone? Install One Thing, tap I have a code, and enter your household code (your partner can find it in their Settings).

Not getting notifications? Go to Settings → Notifications → Send a test. If nothing arrives, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → One Thing.

Delete my account? Settings → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
