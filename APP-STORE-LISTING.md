## App Privacy (the "nutrition label")

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

| Data type | Collected for | Linked to the user? | Used for tracking? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info → **Name** (the first names you type) | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| User Content → **Other User Content** (tasks, events, lists, notes) | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| Identifiers → **User ID** (the random sign-in ID) | App Functionality | Yes | No |

Everything else: **not collected**. There's no location, no contacts, no analytics and no advertising data.

## App Review Information
- **Sign-in required?** **No.** The app creates an anonymous sign-in automatically.
- **Contact info:** your name, phone and email (Apple only uses these if they have questions).
- **Notes for the reviewer** (paste this):

> One Thing is a shared to-do app for two people.
>
> No account is needed. On first launch, tap "Start a household" and enter any two first names. That's the whole setup. To test with one device, choose to be either person; you can add tasks for both.
>
> Native features to try:
> 1. Notifications: Settings (gear icon) → Turn on notifications → Send a test. The app also sends a 7 a.m. task reminder, a 7 p.m. reminder if something's still open, a reminder 1 hour before each event, and alerts when a partner adds or finishes a task.
> 2. The Events tab: tap +, add an event starting a minute from now, and once it starts, tap "We went" to earn Stars.
> 3. The Home tab: an animated room that changes from day to night and grows as you complete tasks.
>
> Account deletion: Settings → Delete my account (at the bottom).
> Privacy policy and terms: Settings → Privacy.
